ABOUT DROPSTACK

Open source, without the noise.

DropStack helps people discover dependable open source alternatives to proprietary software. The directory stays simple, useful, and community driven.

Our approach

DropStack focuses on useful software that gives people real ownership, flexibility, and a path away from expensive proprietary tools. Every listing is managed from one simple admin panel so the public site stays fast and easy to maintain.

Drop information can be added and updated through the Admin Panel. Published Drops appear on the Drops page, while published news appears on the News page.
